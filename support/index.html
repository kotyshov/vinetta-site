Vinetta Support

Vinetta helps you decide what to drink: pair food with wines from your own collection, or photograph a restaurant wine list and get the right glass for your dish.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Vinetta works immediately — no sign-up, no login.

Why does a pairing sometimes take a few seconds?

Recommendations are generated by an AI model. A typical answer arrives in 5–15 seconds.

Is there a limit on pairings?

Vinetta includes a generous monthly allowance of AI actions (pairings, label scans, list scans). If you ever hit it, it resets at the start of the next month.

Where is my wine collection stored?

On your device — and, if you're signed into iCloud, in your private iCloud storage so it syncs across your devices. The developer has no access to it.

The label scan got something wrong.

Scanning works best with a straight, well-lit photo of the front label. You can always edit any detail after scanning.

How do I rate a wine?

Swipe right on a bottle and mark it Consumed — Vinetta asks “How was it?” right away. You can also rate any time from the wine's detail page.
